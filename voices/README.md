# Voice Cloning Reference Audio (voices/)

Drop your reference audio samples here to clone ANY voice for your videos!

### Guidelines:
1. **Length**: 10 to 30 seconds of speech.
2. **Quality**: Clean audio with minimal background noise or music.
3. **Format**: \.wav\, \.mp3\, \.m4a\, or \.ogg\.
4. **Naming**: Name it after the speaker, e.g.:
   - \sample.wav\ (default)
   - \illage_chacha.wav\
   - \my_voice.mp3\

### How to use in any project:
- Drop your sample into this folder (\oices/sample.wav\) or into your project's \oices/\ subfolder.
- Run:
  \\\ash
  python scripts/clone_voice.py <project_folder>
  \\\
  Or just ask me in chat: *"Clone the voice from sample.wav for this video!"*
