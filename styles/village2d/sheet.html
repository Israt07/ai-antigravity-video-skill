5 · Production & Pipeline Rules
  • Line = Scene: Every script line is a self-contained scene with its own cached audio, visual code, and rendered clip.
  • Dialogue Bubbles: Crisp white rounded speech bubbles with bold dark text and a tail pointing to the current speaker.
  • Filmic Grain & Vignette: Subtle 3.5% organic film grain and 25% warm lens vignette framing the daylight scene.
  • Seamless Concat: The final video is an exact concatenation of per-scene clips (`out/scene_01.mp4` … `out/scene_XX.mp4`).
